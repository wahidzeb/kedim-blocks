About Kedim Blocks

Kedim Blocks is a calm block puzzle with Kedim the orange cat. At the bottom of the screen you get three clay blocks in different shapes: a single square, short bars, small corners, a big square and more. Drag a block onto the 8 by 8 board, and when a whole row or column is full it pops away with a happy meow.

The game ends when none of your blocks fit, so the real skill is keeping enough room for whatever comes next. There are three ways to play: 40 Adventure levels with fish to catch, a Daily picture board and an Endless mode for high scores.

How to play

Drag one of the three clay blocks from the bottom onto the 8 by 8 board and let go where it fits. Fill a whole row or column and it pops away. When all three blocks are used, three new ones arrive. A block that cannot fit anywhere turns grey, which is your warning to make room.

Tips from Zaib

  1. Look at all three blocks before you drop the first one, and think about where the other two will go.
  2. Keep the middle open for the big square and the long bars, and fill the edges and corners first.
  3. Do not leave single holes, and in fish levels build rows and columns that run through the fish.

Good to know

There is nothing to buy and no timer. Your levels, best scores and stars are saved on your device.
